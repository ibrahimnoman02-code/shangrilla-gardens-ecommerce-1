# SHANGRILLA GARDENS
## Social Media Design System & Visual Style Guide
**Core Pillars:** Consistency • Creativity • Appetite Ignition

---

### 1. Brand Essence & Aesthetic Direction
The **Shangrilla Gardens** visual identity is built on **"Regal Chiaroscuro"**—the dramatic contrast between deep, moody, blackish-charcoal shadows and warm, fiery, golden highlights. 

* **Goal 1: Ignite Hunger.** Every image must make the viewer practically smell the char, feel the heat of the sizzler, and crave the juicy glaze of the meat.
* **Goal 2: Unmistakable Consistency.** A follower scrolling through their feed should recognize a Shangrilla Gardens post within $0.5\text{ seconds}$ before even seeing the logo, achieved through locked-in colors, Times New Roman-style serifs, and dark ambient lighting.
* **Goal 3: Dynamic Creativity.** Consistency does **not** mean using the exact same layout every day. We use a modular system of rotating post formats, angles, and atmospheric effects so the feed feels alive and exciting.

---

### 2. Color Palette (Dark, Moody & Appetite-Driven)
The background and negative space must remain predominantly in the **Blackish / Charcoal** spectrum so the warm tones of the food (reds, oranges, golden browns, and fresh greens) pop aggressively off the screen.

| Color Role | Color Name | Hex Code | RGB | Usage Guidelines |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Background** | **Onyx Black** | `#0D0E10` | `13, 14, 16` | Deep shadow areas, vignette edges, and main dark backdrops. |
| **Secondary Surface** | **Charcoal Shadow** | `#1F2226` | `31, 34, 38` | Matte black plates, cast-iron sizzlers, and slate table surfaces. |
| **Ambient Accent** | **Deep Obsidian Teal** | `#0A2226` | `10, 34, 38` | *Subtle background fabric/drapery only* (max $15\%$ of frame) to create depth behind warm food. |
| **Primary Brand Accent** | **Royal Gold** | `#D4AF37` | `212, 175, 55` | Primary headlines, logo metallic sheen, dividers, and royal crests. |
| **Secondary Text / Highlight** | **Champagne Cream** | `#F3E5AB` | `243, 229, 171` | High-contrast dish titles, sub-headlines, and gradient stops for gold text. |
| **Hunger Trigger Accent** | **Fiery Amber / Sizzle** | `#E85D04` | `232, 93, 4` | Warm directional rim lighting on wood boards, meat glaze, and flying ember sparks. |

> **Gold Gradient Specification (for Headlines & Borders):**
> Linear Gradient at $135^\circ$: `#B38728` ($0\%$) $\rightarrow$ `#FBF5B7` ($50\%$) $\rightarrow$ `#AA771C` ($100\%$).

### 3. Typography System
Your typography bridges classic royal heritage with sharp modern readability.

#### A. Primary Display Headline (Dish Names & Hero Titles)
* **Typeface:** **Times New Roman** (Bold / Regular) or high-contrast classic serif equivalents (*Cinzel Decorative*, *Playfair Display SC*, or *Cormorant Garamond*).
* **Case:** ALL CAPS for hero launches (e.g., `ROYAL MUTTON CHOPS`); Title Case for signature classics (e.g., `Shahi Beef Kabab`).
* **Letter Spacing (Tracking):** $+30$ to $+60$ for ALL CAPS; $+0$ to $+10$ for Title Case.
* **Color & Effect:** Champagne Cream (`#F3E5AB`) or Royal Gold Gradient with a subtle outer drop shadow (`#000000`, Opacity $65\%$, Blur $12\text{px}$, Distance $4\text{px}$) to ensure $100\%$ legibility over dark backgrounds.

#### B. Pre-Headers & Sub-Headlines (Category & Taglines)
* **Typeface:** Clean Geometric Sans-Serif (*Montserrat* or *Lato* Light/Medium) **OR** Italicized Times New Roman (`presenting`).
* **Style:** Wide-tracked ALL CAPS (`INTRODUCING`, `BOLD FLAVORS • PREMIUM CUTS • A ROYAL TREAT`).
* **Tracking:** $+200$ to $+300$.

#### C. Dynamic Callouts & Badges (Promos & Triggers)
* **Typeface:** Expressive Brush Script (* Kaushan Script*, *Caveat Brush*, or *Pacifico*).
* **Usage:** Exclusively inside gold brush strokes, ribbons, or callout badges (e.g., *New Launch*, *Chef's Special*, *Sizzle & Feast*).
* **Color:** Onyx Black (`#0D0E10`) text placed directly on top of a Royal Gold (`#D4AF37`) brush stroke.

### 4. Brand Assets & Graphic Elements
Keep a dedicated folder of transparent PNG/SVG overlays to assemble posts quickly:

1. **The Shangrilla Logo (`[Insert Your Logo File Here]`):**
   * **Placement Rule:** Never place the logo in the dead center. Anchor it in the **Bottom Right** (preferred for dark moody shots) or **Top Left** (when paired with the *"Shangrilla presenting"* lockup).
   * **Clear Space:** Maintain a margin equal to $50\%$ of the logo's height from the edge of the canvas.
2. **Royal Crown Icon:** Small minimalist gold crown placed centered above `"INTRODUCING"` or inside promotional brush badges.
3. **Gold Brush Strokes & Ribbons:** Textured metallic gold paint swipes used as containers for *"New Launch"* or price/offer callouts.
4. **Fine Gold Dividers:** $1\text{px}$ solid gold lines flanking pre-header text or framing the outer border of special announcement posts (inset $24\text{px}$ from canvas edge).
5. **Atmospheric Overlays (PNGs set to `Screen` or `Add` blend mode):**
   * **Ember Sparks:** Floating golden-orange fire particles for BBQ, Tikka, and Kabab posts.
   * **Wispy Steam/Smoke:** Soft, semi-transparent white/warm-grey smoke rising directly from hot meat.

### 5. "The Hunger Triggers": Photography & Editing Checklist
Before adding any text or logos, every food photo must pass this 5-step editing workflow (Lightroom / Photoshop / Camera Raw) to trigger maximum cravings:

1. **The Specular Glaze (Juiciness):**
   * *Dry meat kills appetite.* Use a local adjustment brush on the meat to boost **Whites ($+15$ to $+25$)** and **Texture ($+20$)**. The oil and marinade must glisten under the light.
2. **Warm Rim Backlighting:**
   * Always light the food from the back-side ($10\text{ o'clock}$ or $2\text{ o'clock}$ position) with a warm tungsten/amber gel ($3200\text{K}$). This carves the meat out from the dark background and creates dramatic long shadows across the wooden serving board.
3. **Color Calibration for Craving:**
   * **Reds & Oranges:** Shift Hue slightly toward deep crimson/amber; boost Saturation ($+10$ to $+15$) so char-grilled edges and chili garnishes look fiery and rich.
   * **Greens (Lime, Cilantro, Lettuce):** Shift Hue slightly toward emerald/yellow-green and boost Luminance ($+10$) so garnishes look crisp and freshly cut against the dark meat.
4. **Surface & Prop Consistency:**
   * Stick to **3 core prop textures**: Dark mahogany/teak wooden boards, matte black ceramic plates/cast-iron sizzlers, and gold-and-crimson handle cutlery.
5. **Depth of Field & Vignetting:**
   * Keep the front pieces of meat tack-sharp ($f/2.8 - f/4$ look) while letting the background melt into soft bokeh with warm candle/fairy light orbs. Apply a $-25$ dark vignette to pull the eye straight to the center of the platter.

### 6. Creative Post Templates (Avoiding the "Boring Feed" Trap)
Rotate through these **5 distinct templates** sequentially so your Instagram/Facebook grid looks cohesive yet visually surprising on every scroll:

#### Template 1: "The Grand Reveal" (Hero Launch Format)
* **Best For:** New menu items, signature platters (e.g., *Royal Mutton Chops*).
* **Composition:** $45^\circ$ dining-angle hero shot, centered platter.
* **Typography Layout:** Top-centered hierarchy: Crown Icon $\rightarrow$ `INTRODUCING` flanked by gold lines $\rightarrow$ Large `TIMES NEW ROMAN` Dish Title $\rightarrow$ 3-bullet flavor tagline.
* **Creative Accent:** Angled gold brush-stroke badge on the middle-right (`New Launch`).

#### Template 2: "The Sizzle & Sparks" (Action & Atmosphere Format)
* **Best For:** BBQ, Tikkas, Sizzlers (e.g., *Sultan's Mutton Tikka*).
* **Composition:** Rule-of-thirds placement—platter angled in the bottom-left foreground; blurred restaurant ambiance with warm candle glow in the background.
* **Typography Layout:** Right-aligned, stacked gold serif title in the upper-right negative space.
* **Creative Accent:** Heavy golden ember sparks and spice dust flying upward from the sizzler across the frame; logo anchored in the bottom-right corner.

#### Template 3: "The Editorial Minimalist" (Ultra-Luxury Format)
* **Best For:** High-end kebabs, chef specials (e.g., *Shahi Beef Kabab* / *Turkish Mutton Kabab*).
* **Composition:** Low-angle ($15^\circ - 25^\circ$) eye-level shot with deep black negative space at the top and a subtle fold of dark obsidian-teal velvet in the background.
* **Typography Layout:** Single, bold, uncrowded Title Case serif headline (`Shahi Beef Kabab`) floating cleanly above the dish. No clutter, no badges—pure confidence.

#### Template 4: "The Dual Sensory Split" (Macro + Wide Collage)
* **Best For:** Highlighting texture, steam, and tenderness (e.g., *Tikka Collage*).
* **Composition:** Vertical 2-panel split frame.
  * **Top Panel ($50\%$):** $45^\circ$ full platter view showing the wooden board, chutneys, and gold cutlery.
  * **Bottom Panel ($50\%$):** Extreme macro close-up ($100\%$ zoom) of the charred, glistening meat fibers with rising steam and fresh cilantro.
* **Note:** Can use a warm sandstone/parchment surface (`#D8B898`) occasionally as a "Daytime/Lunch Feast" contrast post while keeping the gold cutlery and dark cast-iron sizzler to maintain brand ties.

#### Template 5: "The Signature Presenter" (Brand-First Format)
* **Best For:** Evergreen menu highlights and carousel cover slides.
* **Composition:** Tight diagonal crop of the sizzler filling $75\%$ of the frame from bottom-left to top-right.
* **Typography Layout:** Top-left lockup featuring the **3D Gold Shangrilla Logo** next to italicized `Shangrilla` $\rightarrow$ `— presenting —` $\rightarrow$ `Dish Name`.

### 7. Pre-Publish Quality Control Checklist
Before exporting any social media graphic, verify:
- [ ] **The Squint Test:** If you squint at the image, are the **Food** and the **Dish Title** the two brightest elements?
- [ ] **Spell Check:** Double-check all dish names (e.g., ensure *Turkish* and *Mutton* are spelled accurately before rendering 3D gold text).
- [ ] **Logo Breathing Room:** Is the Shangrilla logo clean, metallic, and free from overlapping food or busy text?
- [ ] **Moisture & Heat:** Does the dish have visible glaze, steam, or warm ember glow?
